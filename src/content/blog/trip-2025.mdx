Let me introduce you to the car that will be taking me on this grand adventure.

She's a 2007 Hyundai Sonata. I call her "Sunny".

For years, her only purpose in life has been to get me to work and back again &mdash; about a 20 minute drive. 
However, for the next month, she will be driving thousands of miles on long interstates, treacherous mountain passes, and terrifying dirt and gravel roads.
I've come to really appreciate what she's capable of, to the extent that now just putting around town now feels like a waste of potential.

However, while she may have a lot of potential, what she doesn't have is trunk space.
